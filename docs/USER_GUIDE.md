# Forensic Examiner User Guide

## Step 1: Sign In
Choose your investigator persona or supply your authorized credentials.

## Step 2: Investigation Case
Create a new case or activate an ongoing investigation from the Cases tab.

## Step 3: Evidence Ingestion
Drop raw logs into the Evidence dropzone. DCSRE automatically hashes the file with SHA-256 and detects the appropriate parser.

## Step 4: Timeline Analysis
Review the incident progression in the Timeline tab. Toggle "Attack Path Only" to filter background noise.

## Step 5: Attack Graph
Explore the network topology, compromised hosts, and lateral movement in the Attack Graph tab.

## Step 6: Detection Alerts
Inspect MITRE ATT&CK techniques in the Detections tab. Mark benign events as false positives with an audit note.

## Step 7: Report Generation
Export the final court-admissible forensic PDF or HTML report from the Reports tab.
